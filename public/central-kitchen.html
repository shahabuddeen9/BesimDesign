Specialized Commissary Engineering

Unlike standard commercial kitchens, central commissaries require complex environmental containment, heavy utility loads, and rigorous food sanitation architecture.

❄️

Sub-Zero Cold Chain & Blast Freezing

Custom-engineered walk-in chillers (+2°C to +4°C) and -40°C blast freezers with 100mm to 150mm PUF thermal insulation panels, pressure release valves, and integrated IoT data logging to preserve food safety standards.

🛡️

Cleanroom Hygiene & Separation Zoning

Physical architectural barriers separating raw delivery loading, vegetable peeling, butchery, cooked food packing, and vehicle dispatch to eliminate cross-contamination risks and comply with HACCP and FSSAI guidelines.

🌪️

Heavy Commercial Exhaust & Ecology Scrubbers

High-CFM stainless steel hoods engineered with internal baffle grease filters, electrostatic precipitators (ESP), and water wash wet scrubbers for 99% smoke and odor containment before rooftop discharge.

🚰

HACCP Drainage & Grease Separation

Continuous Grade 304 stainless steel trench drains with removable silt basket strainers, rounded cove profiles for sanitization, and multi-chamber high-temperature grease interceptors.

⚡

Commercial Gas & MEP Utility Distribution

Heavy-gauge seamless copper or C-PVC gas manifolds with gas leak detection sensors, automated solenoid shutoff valves, Ansul R-102 fire suppression, and synchronized 3-phase power distribution.

🏭

Factory Fabricated Stainless Steel Millwork

Food-grade stainless steel prep tables, shelving, boiling pan surrounds, and sinks fabricated directly at Besim Design’s Nalasopara factory for precision tolerances and rapid on-site commissioning.
