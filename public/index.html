Technical processes, delivery guarantees, and statutory clearance procedures.

For boutique hospitality spaces (3,000–6,000 sq.ft), architectural concept to tender documentation takes 3 to 5 weeks. Fit-out execution runs 8 to 14 weeks depending on kitchen MEP complexity. For large industrial PEB builds, turnaround averages 5 to 7 months.

We provide both models. Our Turnkey Delivery package covers end-to-end design, municipal clearances, procurement, MEP contracting, and site supervision with single-point accountability. We also offer Design & PMC consulting for clients with existing general contractors.

Commercial hospitality requires specialized engineering. Our in-house MEP engineers design kitchen exhaust scrubbers, fresh-air intake balancing, grease traps, fire suppression (NFPA / NBC norms), and bar plumbing to guarantee compliance with municipal and health authorities on first inspection.

Our principal studio handles projects nationwide. We have completed active hospitality and industrial projects in Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Goa, Udaipur, Pune, and Hyderabad with dedicated on-site project management teams.
