Our Heritage

Welcome to BESIM DESIGN

Architect and Turnkey Interior studio based in Ghatkopar West, Mumbai, Maharashtra.

Bridging Architectural Dreams & Precision Execution Since 1992

Besim Design was established 20+ years back in the year 1992. We realized that unless there is a very strong and dedicated team to execute, most designs remain dreams. Here we took a conscious call to create our own dedicated teams to execute projects.

Realizing the merit and success in this we took it ahead and extended our services to other designers making it easier for them to get more creative and focus their time on purely design rather than the general paraphernalia. Here Besim Design the turnkey solution company was born.

Over the years we have exactly understood the detailed needs of designers and the limitations of clients and learned to create harmony for all. Besim Design is in complete gratitude to all the designers and the clients we work with and look at creating long term relationships of trust and joy with all of them.

Founder & Managing Director

Mr. Sanjay Baikerikar

GD Arch, Academy of Architecture (1992 Batch)

After completing architecture at the academy, Mr. Sanjay Baikerikar worked with ABM and associates for a year under Alfaz sir and Alankar sir. Then starting his own firm initially named lemongrass which was a design studio, he realized the importance of execution.

Approaching turnkey as an architect made a huge difference. The importance of design, details, and finishing over cutting corners and only making profits was the reason to get into turnkey. His friend Sameep Padora (Director of SP+A) asked him to assist with a complicated project at Phoenix (Kurla) which was successfully executed. From here started the journey of executing turnkey landmarks with unparalleled management.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear insights into our 20+ years of turnkey delivery, in-house factory, and MEP standards.

Besim Design was established 20+ years back in 1992 as a design studio under Managing Director Sanjay Baikerikar (GD Arch, Academy of Architecture). We realized that unless there is a strong and dedicated team to execute, most designs remain dreams. We created our own dedicated teams to provide complete turnkey execution from design to handover.

We operate our own state-of-the-art furniture manufacturing facility in Nalasopara, on the outskirts of Mumbai. We initiate the production of all loose and site-specific furniture items at the factory itself, accelerating project timelines while maintaining superior fit, finish, and structural quality.

Our key USP is our ability to deliver complete turnkey solutions—including MEP services (Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing) and consultancy. This integrated approach enables us to streamline project timelines while maintaining the highest standards of quality and finish.

We make accurate upfront costings and continually update costings on any design or specification changes so clients can make conscious decisions on escalations. At project completion, we provide comprehensive site handover files with renovation details, work-in-progress photos, and emergency contact numbers.
